TASKILL Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-24 · Version 1.2

1. Who We Are

TASKILL (“TASKILL,” “we,” “us”) is a habit and routine management app for iOS, developed and operated by an individual developer. The developer’s name as shown on the App Store listing is the operator’s name.

  • Contact: use the contact form (see Section 5.2). You can also reach it from Settings > Support > Contact in the app.
  • Data protection officer / local representatives: none. TASKILL is not offered in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or Canada (Section 2), and we do not run a server that holds your data (Section 3).

2. Scope and Where TASKILL Is Offered

This policy applies to the TASKILL iOS app and to the website that hosts this document. TASKILL is available in English only, for iOS only.

TASKILL is offered on the App Store in Japan, the United States, Australia, and New Zealand only. This policy is written for users in those countries. We will revise it before adding any other country whose law it does not already cover.

3. Your Habit Data Stays on Your Device

Everything you create in TASKILL (routines, tasks, goals, schedules, execution history, statistics, and settings) is stored only on your device, using Apple’s SwiftData framework. TASKILL has no account and no sign-in. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or backs up this data, and TASKILL’s own code does not send it anywhere.

Reminders and notifications TASKILL shows are scheduled on your device by iOS (local notifications). Widgets and Live Activities read the same on-device data. None of this leaves your device.

What can leave your device is limited to four things: communication by the advertising SDK (Section 6), crash reports sent by Firebase Crashlytics (Section 5.3), usage analytics sent by Google Analytics for Firebase (Section 5.4), and the contact form if you choose to use it (Section 5.2). A backup file you export yourself (Section 4) goes only where you put it.

4. Backing Up and Restoring Your Data

Settings > Data lets you export and import a backup.

  • Export backup writes all your TASKILL data (routines with their hierarchy and order, tasks, goals, schedules, execution records, and a few settings) into one JSON file. iOS asks you where to save it. TASKILL itself does not upload the file anywhere and does not encrypt it, so anyone who can open the file can read its contents.
  • If you save the file to a cloud location such as iCloud Drive, or to another provider’s storage that appears in the Files app, that provider stores and handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy, not under ours. We do not receive a copy.
  • Import backup reads a file you pick, checks it, and, after you confirm, replaces the data currently in the app with the file’s contents. The file is read on your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else.

5. Information We Receive or That Is Sent to Others

5.1 Advertising (Google AdMob)

See Section 6 for details of what Google receives and your choices.

5.2 Contact Form

Settings > Support > Contact opens a Google Form. The form does not require you to sign in to Google and does not automatically collect your email address.

  • What we receive: what you type into the form (your message and the email address you enter if you want a reply), plus the TASKILL app version (and build number) and the iOS version, which the app fills in for you and which you can edit or delete before sending. We do not fill in anything else from your device.
  • Why: to read and answer your question or request, and to understand which versions are affected.
  • Where it is stored: the form is provided by Google, so your response is stored on Google’s servers in the developer’s Google account, under Google’s privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google may also process technical data (such as your IP address) when you open and submit the form.
  • Replies: we reply to the email address you wrote in the form, from the operator’s email address.
  • How long we keep it: We try not to keep information longer than necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected.
  • Please do not include sensitive information in the form. You can ask us to delete your submission by writing to us again through the form.

5.3 Crash Reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

TASKILL uses Firebase Crashlytics, a crash-reporting service provided by Google, to learn when the app crashes so we can investigate and fix the problem. TASKILL also uses Google Analytics for Firebase, described in Section 5.4.

  • What is sent: when the app crashes, Crashlytics receives the default crash information: the stack trace, the state of the app at the time of the crash, device and operating-system information (such as device model and iOS version), and a Crashlytics installation identifier that distinguishes installations of the app. Because TASKILL also uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Section 5.4), a crash report also carries the most recent Analytics events recorded before the crash (a trail of which screens you opened and which features you used, as listed in Section 5.4). If you turn off Usage analytics, those events are no longer recorded.
  • What we do not send: TASKILL does not attach a user ID, custom log messages, or custom keys to crash reports, and does not give Crashlytics your routines, tasks, or other habit data. TASKILL has no account, so crash reports are not linked to who you are.
  • Why: to investigate crashes and improve the app’s quality. We do not use crash reports for advertising or tracking.
  • Provider and retention: the service is provided by Google. According to Google’s Firebase documentation, crash stack traces and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days.
  • More information: Firebase privacy and security information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and Google’s Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

5.4 Usage Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

TASKILL uses Google Analytics for Firebase, an analytics service provided by Google, to learn which screens and features are used so we can decide what to improve. It is on by default and you can turn it off (see “Your choice” below).

  • What is sent: only these events. When you open a screen (Home, Routines, a routine’s detail or editor, Tasks, Calendar, Goals, Settings, an execution screen, the session summary, the execution log, or Acknowledgements), TASKILL records which screen it was. TASKILL also records that you used a feature: creating, editing, or deleting a routine; starting, completing, or ending an execution session; creating a task; exporting or importing a backup; and turning one of these Settings on or off (start-time reminders, pause notifications, voice announcements, countdown before start), together with whether you turned it on or off.
  • What is not sent: TASKILL does not send the names or contents of your routines, tasks, goals, or notes, or anything else you type, and does not send counts or durations. It does not set a user ID or user properties. TASKILL has no account, so this data is not linked to who you are.
  • Automatic information: Firebase adds some information on its own: an app instance identifier (a random ID generated for each installation of the app, which is deleted when you uninstall), device and iOS information, the app version, and approximate location such as country derived from the IP address, together with Firebase’s standard automatic events (for example, first open, session start, and app update).
  • Advertising features are off: TASKILL uses the version of the Firebase SDK that does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA), and turns off Google signals, linking with advertising, and ad personalization for Analytics. The analytics data is not used for advertising or tracking, and is not combined with the data of the advertising SDK in Section 6.
  • Why: to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • Your choice: Settings > Privacy > Usage analytics turns this off or on. When it is off, TASKILL stops collecting Analytics data. The setting is kept on your device and is not changed by Delete All Data.
  • Provider and retention: the service is provided by Google under its own terms. Google keeps event data for the retention period set for TASKILL’s Analytics property, which is 14 months. See Firebase privacy and security information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy), Google Analytics data retention (https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196), and Google’s Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

5.5 Apple

Downloading TASKILL from the App Store, and any crash or analytics reports you choose to share with developers through iOS settings, are handled by Apple under its own privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not control that.

6. Advertising and Tracking Choices

TASKILL is free and shows ads. It uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is included in the app). Ads appear as banner and full-screen (interstitial, including video) ads. Apart from the crash-reporting and usage-analytics services in Sections 5.3 and 5.4, TASKILL itself does not use any other analytics or tracking service.

6.1 What Google may receive and collect

The SDK communicates directly between your device and Google’s servers. It does not pass through any server we operate, and TASKILL does not give the SDK your routines, tasks, or other habit data. Depending on your choices below, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking (Section 6.2);
  • IP address, and coarse location derived from it;
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, clicked, or closed) and product interaction in ads;
  • diagnostics, performance, and crash data for the SDK.

Google uses this data to serve, measure, and limit the frequency of ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and to improve its services. How Google handles it is described in Google’s pages:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/privacy/data-disclosure

6.2 App Tracking Transparency and personalized ads

On first launch, after the notification permission, TASKILL shows a short explanation and then Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.

  • If you allow tracking: Google can use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to show ads that match your interests (personalized ads) and to measure ads, which may include linking with data from other companies' apps and websites that use Google’s services. This is the only tracking in TASKILL. TASKILL’s own code does not track you.
  • If you do not allow tracking (or tracking is restricted on your device): the SDK does not use the IDFA, and TASKILL asks Google to show non-personalized ads only. You still see ads; they are just less relevant.

You can change your choice at any time: in TASKILL, Settings > Privacy > Personalized ads shows your current state and opens iOS Settings; or go to iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn tracking off or on for TASKILL. TASKILL applies the change the next time it comes to the foreground.

“Do Not Track” is a web-browser signal. TASKILL is an app and does not respond to it; the iOS tracking choice above is the control we honor.

6.3 No consent-management prompt

Because TASKILL is not offered in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, it does not show a Google consent-management (UMP) prompt. We will add one before offering TASKILL in those regions.

6.4 Our responsibility

Using a third-party advertising SDK does not reduce our responsibility for what it collects. We disclose it here and in the App Store’s App Privacy information, and we will keep both accurate if the SDK changes.

7. How We Use Information

We use the information in Section 5 only to answer your questions and fix problems you report, to investigate crashes, to understand which features are used and improve the app, and (through Google) to show and measure ads. We do not use it for profiling beyond that, and we do not sell it to anyone.

8. Sharing, and Notice for U.S. State Laws

We share information only with the parties described in Sections 5 and 6 (Google, for ads, crash reports, usage analytics, and the contact form), and where required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property.

We do not sell your information for money. If you allow tracking, sharing of your device identifier with Google for personalized advertising may count as a “sale” or “share” under some U.S. state privacy laws (for example, California’s). You can opt out at any time by turning off tracking for TASKILL in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking (Section 6.2). People who have not allowed tracking are treated as opted out: TASKILL signals Google to restrict data processing for them.

9. International Transfers

Google processes data in many countries, including outside your own country of residence, under its own policies and safeguards. We do not transfer your habit data anywhere, because we never receive it.

10. Retention and Deletion

  • Your TASKILL data stays on your device until you delete it. In TASKILL, Settings > Delete All Data removes everything on the device; uninstalling the app removes the app’s data too. Exported backup files are kept where you saved them. Delete them yourself.
  • Crash reports are retained by Google for the period stated in Section 5.3.
  • Usage analytics are retained by Google for the period stated in Section 5.4. Turn off Settings > Privacy > Usage analytics to stop further collection. Because the data is not linked to who you are, we cannot look up or delete the data of one person.
  • Ad data held by Google follows Google’s retention policies. Resetting or turning off tracking stops further use of the IDFA for TASKILL.
  • Contact form submissions are kept as stated in Section 5.2.

11. Your Choices and Rights

  • Turn personalized ads off or on in iOS Settings (Section 6.2).
  • Turn usage analytics off or on in Settings > Privacy > Usage analytics (Section 5.4).
  • Delete your data in the app (Section 10) or export a copy (Section 4).
  • Because we hold almost no personal information about you (only what you send through the contact form), requests mostly concern a form submission: ask via the form to see or delete it.
  • Rights under the laws of Japan, Australia, New Zealand, and U.S. states (such as access, correction, deletion, and opt-out of sale or sharing) apply where those laws give them to you. Ad data held by Google is requested from Google directly.
  • For a question or complaint, contact us (Section 15). You may also contact your local privacy authority.

12. Children

TASKILL is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us information, contact us (Section 15) and we will delete it. TASKILL does not ask your age.

13. Security

Your data is stored on your device and protected by your device’s own security (passcode, encryption). We do not operate a server holding it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Exported backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

14. Changes to This Policy

We update the “Effective date” and “Version” at the top whenever we change this policy. Minor changes (typos, wording, contact details) just update this page. Material changes (for example, adding a new advertising or analytics SDK or a new use of tracking, offering TASKILL in new countries, starting to receive your data on a server, or introducing sign-in or cloud sync in a future version) will also be shown in the app. If TASKILL adds sign-in in a later version, we will revise this policy before it ships. This document’s version history is in its public repository.
